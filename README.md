# abendkasse

Gig done. Invoice sent.

abendkasse writes invoices for DJs after the gig. You type in only what changes ([venue](docs/glossary.md#venue), event, date, times, fee) and press one button. abendkasse fills in everything else, generates the PDF, saves it with your other invoices and opens your email with the PDF attached. You review and send.

It is a free, installable web app ([progressive web app](docs/glossary.md#pwa)) for Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone. No app store, no account, no server: your data stays on your device.

## Status

Requirements stage, not usable yet. The [first release](docs/glossary.md#first-release) serves German [performers](docs/glossary.md#performer) under the [small-business rule](docs/glossary.md#small-business-rule) (§19 UStG).

- [Requirements](docs/requirements.md): what the first release must do, and the decisions still open.
